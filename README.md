**v0.97.58 · Commissioner League Moments**

## Changes in v0.97.58

- Connects the 1976 ABA–NBA merger to the 1975–76 offseason before opening the 1976–77 season. A Viewer uses the historical four-team proposal; a Commissioner selects one to six ABA teams.
- Provides a recovery action in League Office for existing 1946-started saves that have already passed 1976 without recording the merger.
- Imports eligible ABA players without cloning a player who already exists in the NBA universe. Available excluded ABA players can enter the free-agent pool.
- Pauses a Commissioner's next-season transition when a scheduled expansion is due, allowing the Commissioner to select available markets and team identities or postpone the expansion.
- Adds persistent Commissioner relocation alerts to League Office and Franchise Health, with a clear-alert action. An alert never moves a team by itself.
- Keeps the live offseason presentation and synchronizes version metadata, setup banner, and watermark.

## Installation

Replace the repository-root `index.html` and `README.md` with the two files in this ZIP. Keep your existing `portraits/`, `logos/`, and other asset folders. Existing saved universes remain in the browser.

For a save already in 1978, open League Office to restore the missed merger. Commissioners can select the entrant teams there.
