# Basketballverse Historical Portrait Pack 1
## Included with v0.95.06

These image files are bundled locally in `portraits/` and are registered through
Basketballverse's universal portrait resolver. The cropped WebP files are derivatives
of the source photographs listed below.

All sources below were marked PUBLIC DOMAIN IN THE UNITED STATES on Wikimedia Commons
at the time this pack was assembled. Commons notes that copyright status can differ
outside the United States.
