Basketballverse
v0.91.29 · Development By Team

OFFSEASON DEVELOPMENT
- Development no longer lists every player in the league in one huge page.
- A Team dropdown is shown instead.
- The default team is your currently controlled team.
- Choose any other team from the dropdown to review its development.
- Each team view shows its players' old OVR → new OVR and the rating change.
- Team summary shows how many improved and declined.

SCROLLING
- Complete Development → Draft Order is also placed at the TOP of the Development screen.
- You no longer have to scroll through the development results to continue.

VIEWER / UNEMPLOYED
- If there is no controlled team, Basketballverse uses the most recent career team when available, otherwise the first active team.

This build retains the historical cap, luxury tax, repeater tax, and apron systems from v0.91.28.
