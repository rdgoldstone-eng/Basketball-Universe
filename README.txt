Season · Team · League · Front Office · History · Save

SEASON IS THE HOME PAGE
Opening or loading any universe now lands directly on Season.

- Season
- Playoffs
- Offseason

ADVANCE CONTROLS MOVED TO SEASON

- current season
- current phase
- role
- controlled team / entire league
