CHANGES

DECISIONS
- When a simulation stops because a real decision appears, Basketballverse automatically opens the Decisions screen.
- The Decisions subtab no longer displays a "(0)" / "(1)" count next to its name.
- Informational recap items remain excluded from blocking decisions.

TEAM > HEALTH
- Health now opens the injury report, not Franchise Health.
- Controlled Team Injury Report is on top.
- Full League Injury Report is underneath.
- Healthy players are not listed as injuries.
- Injury rows still show status, games missed, injury type, and high fatigue when relevant.

LEAGUE
- Added Franchise Health under League.
- Franchise Health retains attendance, stability, fan support, relocation pressure, and Relocation Watch.

CHAMPION HISTORY
- Championship result is written directly to Season History and Champions immediately when the Finals end.
- Champion is locked again before the next season begins.
- Existing missing champions are repaired from franchise-season championship flags and championship news when possible.
- Newspaper archive is rebuilt after champion repair so champion names no longer remain blank.
- History and Newspapers self-repair when opened.

STARTUP
- Heading now says: Create Your Universe
