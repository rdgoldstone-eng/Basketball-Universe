ERA-AWARE FINANCIAL RULES
- 1983-84: no salary cap, luxury tax, or aprons
- 1984-85: historical salary cap begins
- 2002-03: luxury tax activates
- 2013-14: progressive luxury-tax brackets activate
- 2014-15: repeater-tax status activates
- 2023-24: first and second apron thresholds activate
- 2024-25: full second-apron trade aggregation restriction activates

LUXURY TAX
- Historical tax lines are stored through 2026-27.
- Before 2013-14: $1 tax for every $1 over tax line.
- 2013-14 onward: progressive brackets.
- Repeater teams pay higher bracket rates.
- Tax bills are added to Ownership expenses/cash impact.
- Team tax status is archived each season for repeater calculations.

APRONS
- Front Office shows salary cap, tax line, first apron, second apron and team tier.
- First-apron teams cannot take back more player salary than they send out.
- From 2024-25, second-apron teams cannot aggregate multiple outgoing player salaries in one trade.
- A second-apron team at season end has its first-round pick seven years out marked frozen/non-tradeable in Basketballverse.

CURRENT SAVE
In 1983-84 none of these later-era penalties appear yet. They activate naturally
as the universe reaches the correct seasons.
