Basketballverse
v0.90.2 · Newspaper Archive Fix

NEWSPAPER FIXES
- Every completed season now gets one permanent Basketballverse Chronicle edition.
- The archive rebuilds ALL completed seasons from Season History, so a two-season save should show two papers.
- Missing older papers are repaired automatically on load.
- The paper is refreshed at the END of the offseason so it includes information that did not exist when the Finals ended.

THE PAPER NOW INCLUDES
- Champion and runner-up
- Finals MVP
- MVP
- Rookie of the Year
- Defensive Player of the Year
- Sixth Man
- Top five draft picks
- Free agency / transaction headlines
- Retirements
- Franchise changes, relocation, expansion, rebrands and realignment news
- Top regular-season records when available
- League alignment / playoff format

HISTORY > NEWSPAPERS
- Shows the number of saved editions.
- Has a season selector.
- Renders the selected paper directly instead of copying the old hidden newspaper box.

SAVE REPAIR
Existing saves are repaired from canonical Season History. If two seasons have
been completed and recorded in Season History, two newspaper editions will be
created without requiring another playthrough.

Upload index.html and README.txt to the GitHub Pages repository root.
